Walk with God, one day at a time.

A Catholic Bible and prayer app for iPhone. Every verse word for word.

A short walk with the Lord, every day.

Each day, the Camino gives you a Bible passage, a short thought and a prayer, in about a minute. Then you light a candle. Reading a chapter, praying the Rosary or saying a prayer lights it too. Your candles build a streak with a rest day built in, and no guilt.

The Bible and the prayers are free.

Read the whole Bible, pray the Rosary, keep notes and highlights, and find words for real moments.

Every verse is word for word.

The app shows the exact words from the Bible stored on your phone. The AI helper never writes Scripture.

Your notes and prayers stay on your phone.

No ads. No selling your information.

Also in English and Spanish, in light and dark.
