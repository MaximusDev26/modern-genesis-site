Walk together as a parish

A parish circle lets your people light a candle each day, and see how many candles the parish lights together.

What it is

Each day, people in Modern Genesis read a short passage or say a prayer, then light a candle. In a parish circle, everyone sees one number: how many candles the parish lit today and this week. It is a quiet way to pray side by side through Advent, Lent, or any season.

Only counts, never names

A parish circle shows only counts. Nobody, not even the person who started it, sees who is in it or whose candle is lit. Nothing about what people read or pray leaves their phone.

Start one in three steps

  1. In the app, open Walk together and choose Start a circle, then Parish or group.
  2. Give it your parish's name. You will get a code and an invitation link.
  3. Share the link, or tap Share a poster for the bulletin: a picture with the code and a QR code, ready to print or show on a screen.

Text for the bulletin

Copy this, and add your circle's code:

Walk Advent together. Download Modern Genesis, a free Catholic Bible and prayer app, and join our parish circle with the code ____-____. Each day, read a short passage or say a prayer, then light your candle. We will see how many candles our parish lights together. Only counts are shared, never names.

The Bible, the daily Camino, the Rosary, prayers, today's Mass readings and Walk together are free.
