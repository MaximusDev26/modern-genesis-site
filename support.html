Common questions

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Modern Genesis. Apple handles all billing.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings, then Your data, then Delete everything. Your notes, prayers and progress live only on your phone, so this removes them.

Who runs the app?

Modern Genesis is published by Maximus Development LLC.

Is this an official Church app?

No. It has not been reviewed or approved by the Church. It is a companion for reading and prayer, not a priest or a replacement for your parish.
